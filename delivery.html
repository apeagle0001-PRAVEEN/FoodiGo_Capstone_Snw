Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Most deliveries are completed within 30-45 minutes depending on your location and restaurant preparation time.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery charges vary by location and order value. Many restaurants offer free delivery on orders above ₹300.

Can I track my order?

Yes! You can track your order in real-time from the restaurant to your doorstep using our app or website.

What if my order is late?

If your order is late, contact our support team immediately. We offer refunds or credit for delayed orders.
